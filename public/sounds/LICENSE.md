# Task completion sound

## Asset

| File | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `task-complete.wav` | Soft glass/ceramic tick played after a work item successfully completes |

Replace **only** `task-complete.wav` (same path/name) to change the chime. The Feedback service loads `/sounds/task-complete.wav`.
